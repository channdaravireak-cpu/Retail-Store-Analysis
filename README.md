# Cambodia Retail Store Performance Analysis

A data analysis project exploring sales performance across an 8 branch retail store operating in Cambodia, covering revenue, profit, product performance, and branch consistency from 2023–2025.

## Overview

This project analyzes transaction-level retail data to uncover revenue drivers, profitability patterns, and branch-level performance across categories and regions. The analysis and all accompanying findings, charts, and recommendations are built entirely in Google Sheets.

**View the full analysis:** [Google Sheets Dashboard](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1480ZCaJl9ap6ZFE6pOtja8dHBWjmu2xhXtoLBnRsVj0/edit?usp=sharing)

## Dataset

`cambodia_retail_store.csv` — transaction-level records including:

- Transaction ID and date
- Branch ID, name, city, and region
- Customer ID, age group, and gender
- Product ID, name, category, and brand
- Quantity, unit price, discount percentage
- Revenue, cost, and profit
- Payment method

## Key Findings

- The Revenue by Category chart shows Electronics generating $1,596,703.82, which accounts for 50.1% of the $3,185,794.50 Grand Total. Electronics brings in more revenue than all other six categories combined (Fashion: $476.3k, Sports: $393.2k, Home Living: $314.1k, Grocery: $206.5k, Beauty: $129.5k, Stationery: $69.5k).
- According to the Total Revenue by Branch and Profit by Branch charts, Downtown Plaza ($643,334.45 revenue / $197,233.37 profit) and Central Mall ($635,230.82 revenue / $192,680.52 profit) are the clear top drivers. Conversely, West Gate ($155,405.04) and South Market ($185,979.44) lag furthest behind, bringing in under 11% of combined revenue.
-The Profit Margin by Branch bar chart shows that profitability remains remarkably consistent across every store location.Margins range in a tight band from 30.7% (Downtown Plaza) down to 29.3% (West Gate), indicating identical product mix profitability and cost control across regions.
- 4. The Most Popular Product chart shows revenue heavily concentrated in top tech items, led by Tablet Air 10 ($276,347.91) and Laptop Pro 14 ($214,512.58). The Least Popular Product chart highlights low-ticket grocery and stationery items trailing the list, with Canned Tuna ($1,441.90) and School Backpack ($2,585.97) generating the lowest overall revenue.
- 5. The Monthly Revenue Trend charts for 2023, 2024, and 2025 show clear, repeating seasonality across every branch. very year features a dramatic surge in November and December (peaking around $140k–$170k in monthly revenue), followed by a steep drop to annual lows in February and September.

## Recommendations

1. Put top items front and center: Electronics make up half your total sales. Give top sellers like the Tablet Air 10 and Laptop Pro 14 the best display spots in every store.									
2. Cross Selling: Train staff to recommend phone cases, chargers, screen protectors, and power banks whenever someone buys a phone or laptop. These small add-ons make high profit margins.									
3. Change the product mix: West Gate and South Market bring in very little sales (less than 11% combined). Instead of stocking them with pricey electronics, stock them with daily essentials that local shoppers buy regularly.									
4. Stock up before the holiday: Get extra electronics in stock by October so you do not run out of inventory during the massive November–December which is Christmas and New Year.									
5. Bundling for Khmer New Year: Create easy-to-buy gift bundles in March and April to capture holiday shoppers.									
6. Clear out low sellers: Items like Canned Tuna, School Backpacks, and Pens bring in almost no money. Drop the items that sit on shelves for too long to free up space.									
7. Bundle slow items: Pair slow moving products with popular electronics
